Title:
Shipped v0.6.0 of ketch, my open-source installer. Here's the plan to make it sustainable

Body:

I've been building ketch, a tool that installs command-line apps straight from their GitHub releases on macOS, Linux and Windows. No formulas, checksums verified, one-command rollback.

    ketch install listepo/AirTalk

Where it stands:
- v0.6.0 just shipped: https://github.com/listepo/ketch/releases/tag/v0.6.0
- Single Rust binary, open source at https://github.com/listepo/ketch
- Released under a triple license: GPLv3, a royalty-free license for proprietary desktop, mobile and web apps, or a commercial license

The plan for sustainability: ketch stays free for individuals, and companies get a paid tier with support and team features. The commercial license covers businesses that want to use it without the GPL or attribution terms.

I'd love input from people who've monetised developer tools. What did teams actually pay for first: support, hosted features, or licensing?
